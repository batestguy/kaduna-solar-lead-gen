The MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the application code in `app.py` and
the supporting scripts.

are subject to the ODbL. Any redistributed extract of those features must carry
ODbL attribution and share-alike terms. Third-party libraries and fonts remain
under their own licences.
